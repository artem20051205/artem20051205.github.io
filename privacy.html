Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

MR Burger: Idle Burger Tycoon ("the game") is made by MR_artemka, an independent developer in the Netherlands ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (money, stations, workers, settings) is saved only on your device. The game has no accounts, and we do not receive your save.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded videos and, now and then, other ads. They are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • how you interact with the ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first, with Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Crash reports

When the game crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Google's Firebase Crashlytics so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains:

  • what went wrong in the game's code (the error and where it happened),
  • the game's version, your device's model, operating system and graphics, and how much memory it had free,
  • a random installation ID made by Crashlytics (not your advertising ID, not your name or account),
  • the time of the crash.

Reports are used only to fix the game and are kept by Google for 90 days. Firebase's privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends your achievements and your scores (lifetime earnings, guests served, prestige stars) to it. Your Play Games name and these scores can appear on the game's leaderboards, as Google Play Games shows them; your Play Games settings decide who can see them. The game works the same without signing in. Google Play Games' privacy policy is Google's: policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics

The game does not use any analytics or tracking of its own beyond the crash reports above. If that changes, this policy will say so before it happens.

Purchases

If the game offers purchases (for example "No Ads"), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be deleted, or object to its use. We keep no data about you ourselves: crash reports carry no name or account and are deleted after 90 days, and the advertising and Play Games data is Google's. You can manage it in your Google account and your phone's settings, and we will gladly help if you write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
